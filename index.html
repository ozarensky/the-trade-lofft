Where the stock goes

The question every retail manager asks in the first meeting. Here it is before the meeting. Three destinations, and we'll tell you which one a given month's collection took.

Stage 1

Collected

Bagged rag from charity shops, pallets of returns and end-of-line from retailers, and whole lots from house clearances — picked up on the agreed day and driven to Chippenham the same day.

Stage 2

Sorted and graded by hand

Bags are opened on the tables at Bumpers Farm. Each item is checked for wear, damage and label, and graded into one of three piles. This is the step that decides everything after it, and it's done by people, not a conveyor.

Stage 3

One of three destinations

Independent resellers

Best-grade pieces go to a vetted group of small resale businesses in the South West who buy from us on a repeat basis.

Trade buyers, by the lot

Wearable mid-grade stock is baled and sold in bulk to trade buyers we have dealt with before. Turnaround is inside a fortnight.

Textile recycling

Damaged, stained or unwearable items go to a licensed UK textile recycler for fibre recovery. Nothing goes to landfill from the unit.

The same bag is worth something different to each of you

Charity shops

Roughly a third of donated clothing never sells on a charity shop floor. Bagged and collected on a fixed day, that stock becomes a per-bag payment on a monthly statement instead of a stockroom problem — with no shop volunteer having to weigh, grade or argue.

Retailers and brands

Customer returns, end-of-season and overstock are written down to nil and sit in a mezzanine. We buy them as a lot, collect in a single visit with our own van, and the write-off comes back as a line of recovered value.

Clearance suppliers

House clearance firms and auction houses in Bath, Swindon and Chippenham need a buyer who takes the whole textile lot, not the best rail — and who is still answering the phone next quarter. We buy on repeat, not one-off.

The charity arrangement, in one sentence

We collect bagged clothing from your shops Monday to Thursday, pay an agreed rate per bag with nothing weighed, and send a statement of bags and routes each month.

That is the arrangement our existing charity routes run on. It is the same one we offer any charity retail team in Wiltshire, Somerset or south Gloucestershire.
